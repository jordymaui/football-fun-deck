Game loop

A World Cup game built for emotion, retention, and repeat play

Football.fun is not traditional fantasy and it is not passive prediction. It is a live World Cup game built around squad-building, picks, market timing, and repeat matchday decisions.

Build squad

Start with 3,300 Gold across favourites, regulars, differentials, and underdogs.

Set picks

Choose 5 picks each round to earn Skill Rating.

Transfer / rotate

React to shocks, eliminations, scarcity, and rising assets between rounds.

Watch + earn

Your picks earn SR while your squad earns Gold from the tournament pot.

Repeat Build squad → Set picks → Watch matches → Earn SR + Gold → Transfer / rotate → Climb divisions

Built for every matchday

Not a set-and-forget team. The loop is designed to pull users back in across the full tournament.

Slide 4
Leaderboards & Leagues

SR leaderboards. Public, private & sponsor leagues.

Football.fun's Skill Rating leaderboard is the competitive backbone of the game. F2P and Pro players compete side by side — same picks, same scoring, same ranking. Leagues layer social and commercial structure on top.

Three league formats

  • Public leagues — open to anyone. Club and nation leagues built around shared identity. Users find their community from day one.
  • Private leagues — invite-only. Created by users for friend groups, WhatsApp chats, colleagues. The primary social growth mechanic.
  • Sponsor leagues — a branded private league for a partner’s audience. Your name, your prize pool, your community — for 64 matches.

Why the shared leaderboard matters

F2P and Pro players are ranked together with [F2P] and [PRO] badges. When a free player finishes above Pro players earning real money, the upgrade sells itself — no ad required.
